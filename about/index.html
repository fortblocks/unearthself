About

Unearthself is the holding philosophy, modality and ecosystem for becoming more fully yourself in the world — rooted in the Canadian Badlands and practised wherever people are ready.

The whole

One source, several pathways

Unearthself is not a single product or programme. It is the quiet centre from which the Echo System, the Human Adaptation Model, Basecamp, Badlands Bootcamp and Haven all emerge.

The intellectual and embodied work lives in The Work. The place-based experiences live in the Badlands. The ongoing relationship lives in Community. Together they form a coherent ecosystem rather than a collection of offerings.

The three pillars

Content · Commerce · Community

Content

The modality itself — Echo System, Human Adaptation Model, Ten Principles — and the writing, practice materials and field notes that keep it alive.

Commerce

Experiences you can book: Basecamp retreats and treatments, Badlands Bootcamp programmes for teams, Haven stays, and the shop.

Community

The living circle of participants, alumni and practitioners who continue the work after the land and support each other in ordinary days.

Place

Why the Badlands

Drumheller and the surrounding Canadian Badlands are not a backdrop. The landscape is part of the method.

Hoodoos, coulees and open sky create a natural container. People arrive differently when the ground under them is this honest. We work with that — for challenge, for rest, and for the kind of presence that only becomes available when the pace slows and the land is allowed to speak.

The physical centre in Drumheller holds the venue, the retail space, and the gathering rooms that serve Basecamp, Bootcamp and Haven.

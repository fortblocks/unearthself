Haven

A softer layer of the same philosophy and place. Short stays for rest, soft landing, and the quiet that the Badlands make possible.

Enquire about a stay

What it is

Hospitality without the noise

Haven is the lightest entry into the Unearthself ecosystem. No programme required — just a well-held room, thoughtful space, and the option to lean into the land or the modality if you want to.

It sits alongside Basecamp and Badlands Bootcamp: same building, same care, different intensity. Come for a night or a few days between other things, or as a deliberate pause.

Ways to stay

Simple options

We keep the structure light so the stay can stay light.

1–3 nights

Quiet night

A room, clean space, and the freedom to rest or wander. Ideal after travel or between more structured experiences.

2–4 nights

Soft landing

A slightly longer stay with optional light touch — a guided walk, a short somatic session, or simply more time in the landscape.

By arrangement

Extended pause

For writers, facilitators, or anyone needing a stretch of uninterrupted time in a place that holds presence without demanding it.

Belonging

Community

A living space for participants, alumni, practitioners and the curious. The work continues after the land — through shared language, practice, and connection.

Why it exists

The modality is not a one-off event

Experiences in the Badlands open something. Community is where that something has room to keep unfolding — without needing constant intensity.

We hold a quiet, intentional space for people who have been through Basecamp, Badlands Bootcamp, or the Work itself, and for those who are simply drawn to the language and want to stay close.

Shared language

The Echo System and Ten Principles become usable markers in ordinary days. Community keeps that language alive and refined.

Ongoing practice

Light-touch gatherings, online sessions, and prompts that support the adaptive work without turning it into performance.

Alumni & practitioners

A place for those who have facilitated or participated to stay connected, exchange notes, and resource each other.

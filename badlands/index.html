Badlands Bootcamp

Experiential programmes for teams and organisations in the Canadian Badlands. From high-energy getaway days to deeper, modality-aligned work that stays with people long after they leave.

View packages

Why here

The land does half the work

Drumheller’s hoodoos, coulees and open sky create a natural container. People arrive differently when the landscape is this honest. We use that — for challenge, for reflection, and for shared experience that builds real trust.

Badlands Bootcamp sits inside the wider Unearthself ecosystem. Some programmes stay light and energetic. Others draw directly on the Echo System and Human Adaptation Model so the insights become usable back at work.

On the ground

Activities rooted in Drumheller

We work with the real landscape and the real town — not a generic team-building backdrop.

Hoodoos & coulees

Short hikes and orientation walks among the signature formations. Perfect for opening a day or creating shared reference points.

Valley cycling

Road and trail options through the Badlands and along the Red Deer River. E-bikes available so mixed-ability groups can ride together.

Trail Quest games

Designed challenges that require navigation, collaboration and real-time decision-making — then a structured look at the patterns that showed up.

Venue & retail hub

Our Drumheller base includes gathering space, a small retail area with Badlands Bootcamp kit, and room for briefings and debriefs.

Local partnerships

We can layer in Royal Tyrrell Museum visits, Atlas Coal Mine, or other regional experiences when they serve the programme.

Evening containers

Simple, well-held evenings — food, conversation, optional light practice — so the day has a clear arc.

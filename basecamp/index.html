Basecamp

A restorative retreat centre in the Canadian Badlands. Intimate stays, treatments, and gatherings grounded in the Unearthself modality and the land itself.

Enquire about a stay

The place

A quiet centre in Drumheller

Basecamp is the wellness and hospitality heart of Unearthself. Housed in a carefully restored building in Drumheller, it offers a warm, grounded space for rest, reflection and small-group experiences.

The interiors mix terracotta, dark wood, soft textiles and quiet corners — designed for unhurried conversation, treatment work, and the kind of presence that only arrives when the pace slows. Outside, the Badlands begin within minutes.

What we host

Retreats & gatherings

Basecamp is available for curated private and small-group experiences. Each is held within the Unearthself philosophy.

2–4 nights · Couples

Couples retreats

Quiet, well-held time for two. Shared practices from the Echo System and Human Adaptation Model, space for conversation, and optional paired treatments.

2–3 nights · Women

Women’s gatherings

Intimate groups for friends, circles, or stagette-style celebrations that actually restore. Land-based practices and a pace that honours the group.

1–5 nights · Private

Private & custom stays

Book the centre for your own group, facilitation, or simply a deep rest. We can shape the container with you or leave the space open.

Treatments

Wellness offerings

Bodywork, somatic sessions and quiet practices available as part of a stay or as day visits by arrangement.

Gentle, body-led sessions that help the nervous system settle and patterns become visible.

Restorative bodywork

Echo reflection

Nature attunement walks

Guided time in the Badlands with simple practices for presence and land connection.

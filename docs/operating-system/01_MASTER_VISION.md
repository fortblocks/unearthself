# Master Vision

**Version:** 1.0 — 8 September 2026  
**Classification:** Canonical  
**Audience:** Partners, specialist chats, future investors

## One sentence

Unearth Self is a self-orientation modality that begins as a place-based expedition in the Canadian Badlands and becomes a nationally, then internationally, recognisable system for recognising how we adapt — and choosing again.

## Why this exists

Drumheller is not a larger version of Banff. It is a small town of roughly 7,000 people sitting on one of the most psychologically useful landscapes in North America: deep time made visible. A conventional gym, spa and four-room guesthouse cannot win that market on amenities. 176 local Airbnbs already fight for the same tourist. Four rooms at ~25% occupancy is a hospitality problem dressed as a wellness problem.

The move is not “capture more of the existing visitor”. The move is **create a reason to come** that does not currently exist, and simultaneously give locals a membership they can be proud of.

## The stack

1. **Place** — Basecamp (HQ + spa + gathering rooms) and Haven (four suite apartments in downtown Drumheller), with Canalta as overflow for 50–150 person events.
2. **Experience** — Badlands Bootcamp. A 2–5 day cumulative expedition through PACE. Corporate teams first. Stag/hen, sports clubs, family and local cohorts next.
3. **Companion** — Trail Quest. Offline-capable, GPS-aware, quiet. Reveals Runes, holds Echo Mirror prompts, never steals the landscape.
4. **Modality** — Echo System + HAM. Forged in the field, then packaged as an online course and a language people can keep using after they leave.
5. **Brand** — Rugged, archaeological, precise. Coal and fossil. Ember only where a pulse is needed. Runes as living geometry, not decoration.

## What we sell in year one

Three bookable products, one website, one operations desk:

- **Haven stays** — short stays whether or not the guest is on a Bootcamp
- **Spa / Basecamp treatments** — locals, day guests, retreat add-ons (massage, red light, thermal / contrast, etc.)
- **Badlands Bootcamp packages** — Getaway Day, Trail Quest day, Ride & Reflect, Adaptation Lab (2–3 days), custom 2–5 day PACE expedition

Corporate is the occupancy engine. Spa and Haven are the cash-flow floor. Local programmes are the community moat. IP is the multiple.

## What we do not sell

- Therapy
- A personality test
- A Tough Mudder with sage
- An AI retreat
- A finished “life-changing methodology” we have not yet forged

## North star commercial shape

A four-partner team should operate like a company twenty times its size because the systems are better than the headcount. Grok Bots handle first-touch sales, follow-up, CRM hygiene, content drafts and reporting. Humans hold the land, the guests and the judgement.

Within 3–5 years the enterprise should be an attractive acquisition or franchise platform: replicable retreat IP, a documented facilitator system, a digital course, a brand people recognise from a Rune mark alone, and more than one location.

## Design principle for every product

The guest should feel more human and more here, not more digital.

AI is infrastructure. Nature is the offer. Documentation is how a small team stays coherent.

## Job of this workstream

Turn PACE into a bookable, repeatable 2–5 day expedition that a facilitator can run without inventing the day on the morning.

## Locked architecture

**PACE is the path. Unearth Self is the purpose.**

Existence is the evolution of the forged Rune **Presence**. Self-awareness is not a Rune.

### Working principles

- **Play** — the willingness to enter uncertainty with curiosity, contribute before everything is known and discover what becomes possible with others.  
  *What becomes possible when I loosen my need to get it right?*
- **Adaptability** — changing one’s response to meet reality without abandoning what matters.  
  *What response fits the conditions now?*
- **Connection** — the capacity to remain in meaningful contact with self and others while listening, contributing and allowing difference to exist.  
  *How do we make contact without disappearing, dominating or assuming?*
- **Existence** — meeting the here and now as one’s point of contact with life, time, Earth and meaningful action.  
  *How will I meet this moment, and how do I want my existence to imprint the world?*

### Experience loop (every Rune)

1. Reveal the Rune (glyph, name, principle)
2. Enter the experience
3. Meet friction
4. Look into the Echo Mirror
5. Reorient and choose
6. Activate and carry the Rune (physical and/or digital)

The challenge is an environment that makes something visible. It is not a test of who wins.

## Safety — non-negotiable

- Notice, don’t excavate
- No forced dancing, running, disclosure, alcohol, purchase or approaching unbriefed strangers
- Pass on sharing is always available
- Echo notes stay private
- Town partners are collaborators, not props
- Alcohol before thermal circuit is a separate policy decision, default non-alcoholic
- Screening for movement, thermal and bodywork

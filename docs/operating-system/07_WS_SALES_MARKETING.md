# Workstream — Sales & Marketing

**Code:** SALES  
**Status:** Must start before the building is finished  
**Operating model:** Grok Bots do first touch and follow-up. Humans close and host.

## Priority order (this is not optional)

1. Take money and dates for Haven, spa and Bootcamp
2. Fill November–March calendar (the season everybody else abandons)
3. Build a Calgary / Edmonton corporate pipeline
4. Then content, community, merch, national PR

A beautiful site with no booking is a brochure. A booking engine with no story will not close a $18k team offsite. We need both, in that order of engineering, and the reverse order of copy.

## Offers to put on the site this month

- Request / book Haven
- Book a treatment
- Enquire on Getaway Day, Adaptation Lab, PACE Expedition

Pricing is still an open decision (`17` + `14`). Until locked, the site collects qualified enquiries with a published *from* price or “teams of 8–24, 2–3 days”. Do not hide that this costs real money.

## 90-day sales motion

**Days 1–14**

- CRM live (even if it is HubSpot free + spreadsheet). **D04 stays open.** The house book is the working CRM until a partner closes it.
- Enquiry forms write to the house book. `/spring` files a live lead; a matching research-card domain promotes the company.
- `/admin/leads` — long list of Alberta first-wave companies. Not the pipeline. Score 100 = Fit 60 + Access 25 + Timing 15 (Calgary / Edmonton / Red Deer, headcount 80–800, first-wave industries, named buyer, published email).
- Bands: 90+ pursue · 70–89 sequence if sendable · 50–69 watch · <50 do not email.
- Access gate: no named person + published email + source URL = draft allowed, send blocked. Do not invent emails. Do not buy Apollo.
- Grok Bot: qualify, answer FAQs, propose three dates, book a call
- One-pager PDF for corporate
- Seed list of ~30 Calgary / Edmonton / Red Deer target accounts on `/admin/leads`. Scout JSON ingest on that page. Pipeline stays live files only.

**Days 15–45**

- 20 conversations booked
- Two complimentary or heavily discounted pilot slots reserved as case-study engines
- Local spa launch offer for residents
- Photo/video shoot of the actual building and land

**Days 46–90**

- First paid corporate holds
- Weekend Haven occupancy plan for November–January
- Press kit for Calgary Herald / Avenue / Explore Alberta once two pilots exist
- Alumni language prepared (we do not have alumni yet — do not fake a community)

Bots must never invent prices, promise a Rune outcome as a medical result, or book a canyon day without a facilitator flag.

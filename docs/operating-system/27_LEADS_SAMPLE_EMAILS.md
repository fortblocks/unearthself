# 27 Leads: sample outbound emails

Written 18th November for Chris to read and pick a voice. Drafts only. Nothing here goes to a real lead without Chris's approval, and any send goes from chris@badlandsbootcamp.com. Text in [brackets] is an example slot; the live version fills it from that lead's real, sourced company signal. Each runs about 45 seconds read aloud. Companion to `26_LEADS_PRODUCT_EVOLUTION.md`.

Facts used: about ninety minutes from Calgary (live site), groups of six to twenty-four (live site and `07`), first teams spring 2027 (`23`: first groups March 2027), Basecamp for heat and cold (live site). "Canada's most exciting new corporate retreat" is Chris's own claim, written as his belief.

## 1. Landscape and place

Subject: Somewhere new for [Company]'s team

Hi [First name],

I saw [Company] [opened its new Calgary office this fall]. Congratulations. That kind of year usually earns the team a few days together somewhere memorable.

I'm Chris, one of the partners behind Unearth Self in Drumheller. About ninety minutes from Calgary the prairie falls away into the badlands: hoodoos, coulees, and some of the most beautiful country in Canada. It's the land of the dinosaurs, and it's where we're opening what I honestly believe is Canada's most exciting new corporate retreat, with our first teams in spring 2027.

We take groups of six to twenty-four out on a multi-day expedition across that land. I'm biased, of course. It's hard to describe until you're standing in it.

Could I send you a few photos and grab fifteen minutes in the new year?

Chris

## 2. Team experience on the land

Subject: Two days on the land with your team

Hi [First name],

You lead [people and culture at Company], so I imagine you've planned more offsites than you'd care to count. I'd love to show you a new one.

At Unearth Self, a team of six to twenty-four heads out into the Alberta badlands for a multi-day expedition. The days are spent out on rugged ground in the land of the dinosaurs, working out the route together. The evenings end at Basecamp, our place in town for heat and cold. Somewhere in the middle, people start talking to each other differently. That part I can't promise, but I've watched it happen.

We're about ninety minutes from Calgary and welcoming our first teams in spring 2027. We're new, and we're still shaping it, which is why I'd value your take.

Would a short call in January suit you?

Chris

## 3. Recipient's industry or company

Subject: [Company] and the badlands

Hi [First name],

[Company]'s crews spend the year [building across southern Alberta], so your people already know what a hard day outside feels like. I think that makes them exactly the kind of team who'd get the most out of ours.

I'm Chris, from Unearth Self in Drumheller. We're opening a multi-day expedition in the Canadian badlands for groups of six to twenty-four, about ninety minutes from Calgary. It's rugged country, some of the most beautiful in Canada, and the dinosaurs got there first. Spring 2027 is our first season.

I'll admit I'm still a little amazed this landscape sits so close to the city. I'd love to show it to you.

Would you be up for a quick chat after the holidays?

Chris

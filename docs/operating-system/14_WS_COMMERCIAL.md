## Working price posture (to validate)

Historical planning language used figures in the region of CAD 18,000 average for a 3-day corporate-style booking. That is a planning assumption, not a rate card.

Build a proper rate card with:

- Per-person versus whole-team pricing
- Facilitator day rate baked in
- Canalta overflow pass-through
- Shoulder-season versus peak
- Local treatment menu
- Haven BAR and package rate
- Deposit 30–50%, balance 14 days out
- Cancellation that protects winter dates

## Cost lines to track from day one

- Facilitators and FOH
- Practitioners
- Partner activity / permits / transport
- Meals
- Marketing
- Software
- Property operating costs
- Commission if using planners

## Cash rule

No custom itinerary work beyond a one-hour scoping call without a deposit.

## 3-year ambition (directional, not a model)

A Drumheller flagship that is cash-flow positive, with IP clean enough to take to a second landscape. Acquisition attractiveness comes from system + brand + repeatable product, not from four rooms alone.

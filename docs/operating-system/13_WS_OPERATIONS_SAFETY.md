# Workstream — Operations & Safety

**Code:** OPS  
**Job:** The day actually runs. Nobody gets hurt. Dignity stays intact.

## Operating day types

- Leisure stay only
- Spa / day guest only
- Private Bootcamp in house
- Mixed (most dangerous commercially — inventory collisions)

Write a simple rule: when a PACE Expedition is in house, spa day-guest hours shrink and Haven may be held as programme inventory.

## Safety pack (must exist before first paid outdoor day)

- Risk assessment per quest location (Horseshoe Canyon, town, Eleven Bridges, thermal)
- Weather abort criteria
- Guest screening form (mobility, cold-water, pregnancy, injury)
- Facilitator-to-guest ratios
- First-aid and emergency contacts
- Radio / satellite / coverage map
- Incident log template
- Child policy (default: Bootcamp is adult unless a named family product exists)
- Insurance confirmation for adventure activity + premises

## Psychological safety

Copied from product canon and binding here:

- Notice, don’t excavate
- Pass is always available
- No humiliation challenges
- Dunning and any expressive movement are opt-in intensity
- Town interactions use briefed partners

## Staffing sketch

For a 12–24 person PACE Expedition: 1 lead facilitator, 1 support, 1 FOH/Basecamp, spa practitioners as booked, driver if routes require.

## Seasonal reality

Winter is a product, not an apology. Snowshoe, contrast therapy, indoor Play and shorter outdoor windows. Do not sell a July itinerary in January.

## What this is

- **HAM (Human Adaptation Model)** — the deeper explanatory architecture for how humans protect, adapt and grow. Informs challenge design and facilitator understanding. Not taught as a lecture in the corporate MVP.
- **Echo System** — symbolic, somatic language for recognising adaptive responses and recurring patterns. Echoes are mirrors, not types, not diagnoses, not identities.
- **Echo Mirror** — the guest-facing MVP slice. Short reflective process after meaningful moments.

## Mirror sequence (MVP)

1. What actually happened?
2. What arose internally?
3. Observation versus interpretation
4. What impulse or adaptive response appeared?
5. What might that response have been protecting or seeking?
6. Where are you now?
7. What other response is available?

Early versions may use plain language or broad options: control, withdraw, rush, accommodate, perform, resist, reconnect.

## What must not happen

- No clinical claims
- No “you are a [Echo name]”
- No excavation of biography
- No requirement to share Mirror notes
- Avoid: triggered, activated, reactive, dysregulated

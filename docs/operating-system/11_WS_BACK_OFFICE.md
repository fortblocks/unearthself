# Workstream — Back Office

**Code:** OPSYS  
**Job:** Four partners operate like a company with a full ops desk

## What “back office” means here

The internal system of record for:

- Pipeline and CRM
- Holds, deposits, balances
- Room and treatment calendars
- Retreat run-of-show and staffing roster
- Supplier and partner contacts
- Waivers and incident log
- Weekly P&L snapshot
- Guest comms history (including bot threads)

Guests never see this. Staff live in it.

## November minimum viable ops desk

A single shared workspace (Notion, Attio or HubSpot + a spreadsheet is acceptable for 60 days) with these views:

1. **Today** — arrivals, treatments, retreat sessions, who is on shift
2. **Pipeline** — every enquiry, owner, next action, value
3. **Inventory** — Haven nights, treatment slots, Canalta overflow holds
4. **Money** — deposits due, invoices sent, refunds
5. **Risk** — unsigned waivers, weather flags, under-staffed days

If a fact exists only in a WhatsApp thread, it does not exist.

- Occupancy (Haven)
- Treatment hours sold

Keep it to one page. The roadmap workbook holds the longer view.

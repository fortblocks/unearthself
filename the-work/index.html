The Work

The Echo System and Human Adaptation Model form the intellectual and embodied centre of Unearthself.

Echo System

A symbolic and somatic language for recognising adaptive patterns and reclaiming presence.

The Echo System gives form to the invisible. Through geometric markers, somatic signatures and reflective inquiry, it makes the patterns we live by visible, nameable and workable.

Human Adaptation Model

A rigorous yet warmly human framework for understanding how we adapt, protect, and grow.

The Human Adaptation Model maps the ways we organise ourselves under pressure and in safety, revealing adaptive strategies and offering a clear pathway toward more resilient, authentic ways of being.

The Ten Principles

Living geometric markers. Each principle is both a direction and a practice.

Super Self-Awareness

Seeing the patterns as they arise.

Super Empathy

Meeting self and other with precision and warmth.

Unbreakable Integrity

Alignment between what is known, said and done.

Deep Nature Attunement

The body and the land as primary teachers.

Resilient Adaptability

Meeting change without losing centre.

Radical Authenticity

Refusing the performance that costs presence.

Courageous Curiosity

Staying with the question longer than comfort allows.

Harmonious Collaboration

Working with others without abandoning the self.

Mindful Presence

Returning to what is actually here.

Purpose Driven Vision

Letting a larger why organise the smaller choices.
